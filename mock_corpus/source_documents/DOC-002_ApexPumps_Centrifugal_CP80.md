# Product Specification: Apex CP80 End-Suction Centrifugal Pump
**Manufacturer**: Apex Fluid Systems
**Product Line**: CP80 Heavy Duty Industrial Process Pumps
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40151503 (Centrifugal pumps)

## Technical Specifications
- **Max Flow Rate**: 850 GPM (193 m³/h)
- **Max Discharge Head**: 320 ft (97.5 meters / 9.5 Bar)
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -20°C to 180°C (-4°F to 356°F)
- **Max Operating Pressure**: 232 PSI (16 Bar)
- **Motor Power Rating**: 15 HP to 75 HP (11 kW to 55 kW)
- **Pump Speed**: 1750 RPM / 3500 RPM
- **Impeller Type**: Enclosed 316L Stainless Steel Precision Cast
- **Casing Material**: Ductile Iron ASTM A536 / Duplex Stainless Steel CD4MCu
- **Shaft Seal**: Single Mechanical Seal SiC/SiC/FKM
- **Suction/Discharge Flange**: 4" Suction / 3" Discharge ANSI 150#

## Compliance & Standards
- **Design Standard**: ISO 2858 / ISO 5199 / ANSI B73.1
- **Efficiency Standard**: Hydraulic Institute HI 1.3 / EU EcoDesign Directive
- **Hazardous Area Certification**: ATEX Zone 1 & Zone 2 (Ex II 2G Ex h IIC T4 Gb)
- **Quality Certification**: ISO 9001:2015

## Industrial Applications
Chemical process transfer, industrial wastewater management, cooling tower water circulation, and food processing.
