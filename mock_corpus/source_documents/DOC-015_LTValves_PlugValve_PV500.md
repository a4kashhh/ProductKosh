# Technical Datasheet: L&T PTFE-Sleeved Plug Valve
**Manufacturer**: L&T Valves Limited (Larsen & Toubro)
**Series**: PV500 Sleeve-Sealed Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141600 (Industrial Valves - Plug Valves)

## Technical Specifications
- **Valve Size**: 15 mm to 250 mm (1/2 inch to 10 inch)
- **Pressure Rating**: ANSI Class 150, 300 / PN 16, PN 40
- **Max Operating Pressure**: 51 Bar (740 PSI)
- **Operating Temperature**: -29°C to 204°C
- **Body Material**: Ductile Iron IS 1865 / Carbon Steel WCB / Monel 400
- **Plug Material**: 316 Stainless Steel / Hastelloy C
- **Sleeve Material**: Virgin PTFE / Fluorosinter High Temp PTFE

## Compliance & Standards
- **Indian Standard**: BIS IS 9858 / IBR Certified
- **Testing Standard**: API 598 Zero Bubble Leakage
- **Fugitive Emissions**: TA-Luft / ISO 15848-1 Class AH

## Applications
Corrosive chemical handling at Gujarat Alkalies, hydrofluoric acid service, fertilizer plants.
