# Technical Datasheet: KSB Air-Operated Double Diaphragm (AODD) Pump
**Manufacturer**: KSB Pumps Limited India
**Series**: DP100 Metallic AODD Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40151504 (Positive displacement pumps)

## Technical Specifications
- **Fluid Port Connection**: 50 mm (2 inch) BSP Female
- **Max Flow Capacity**: 568 L/min (150 GPM / 34.0 m³/h)
- **Max Air Supply Pressure**: 8.6 Bar (125 PSI)
- **Max Displacement per Stroke**: 1.7 Liters
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -12°C to 107°C
- **Fluid Section Material**: 316 Stainless Steel / Aluminum
- **Diaphragm & Ball Material**: PTFE (Teflon) with Santoprene Backer

## Compliance & Standards
- **Explosion Safety**: PESO Approved Flameproof (Group II 2GD c TX)
- **Sanitary Compliance**: FDA Compliant Materials (21 CFR 177)
- **Quality System**: ISO 9001:2015

## Applications
Chemical transfer at Asian Paints, ink circulation, solvent recovery in Indian pharma hubs.
