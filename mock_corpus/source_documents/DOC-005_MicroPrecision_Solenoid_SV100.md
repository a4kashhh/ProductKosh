# Product Datasheet: Micro Precision Explosion-Proof Solenoid Valve
**Manufacturer**: Micro Precision Calibration India
**Product Series**: SV100 Direct-Acting Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141605 (Solenoid valves)

## Technical Specifications
- **Port Size**: 1/2 inch NPT Female (DN15 / 15mm)
- **Valve Function**: 2/2-Way Normally Closed (NC)
- **Operating Pressure Differential**: 0 to 10.3 Bar (0 to 150 PSI / 10.5 kg/cm²)
- **Flow Coefficient (Cv)**: 4.5
- **Operating Voltage Options**: 24 VDC (8.5 Watt) / 230 VAC 50Hz (11 Watt)
- **Body Material**: 316L Stainless Steel Barstock
- **Seal Material**: FKM (Viton) / PTFE High Temperature
- **Operating Temperature**: -10°C to 120°C

## Compliance & Standards
- **PESO Approval**: PESO / CCOE Approved Flameproof Enclosure (Ex d IIC T5 Gb)
- **Indian Standard**: BIS IS/IEC 60079 Explosive Atmospheres
- **Ingress Protection**: IP67 Weatherproof
- **Safety Rating**: SIL 3 Capable
