# Technical Specification: KSB Pumps SP50 Slurry Submersible Pump
**Manufacturer**: KSB Pumps Limited India
**Series**: SP50 Mining & Slurry
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40151513 (Submersible pumps)

## Technical Specifications
- **Discharge Diameter**: 100 mm (4 inch) Flanged PN16
- **Max Flow Capacity**: 272 m³/h (1200 GPM / 4533 LPM)
- **Max Head**: 42.6 meters (140 ft)
- **Max Solids Passage**: 38 mm spherical solids
- **Motor Rating**: 30 kW (40 HP) 415V 3-Phase 50Hz
- **Operating Temperature**: 0°C to 50°C continuous
- **Impeller Material**: High-Chrome White Iron (Hardness 650 HB)
- **Volute Casing**: High-Chrome Iron with Abrasion Resistant Rubber Liner
- **Mechanical Seal**: Double Mechanical Seal in Oil Chamber (SiC/SiC)

## Compliance & Standards
- **Mine Safety Approval**: DGMS (Directorate General of Mines Safety) Approved for Underground Mines
- **Hazardous Safety**: PESO Approved Ex d IIB T4 Mb
- **Enclosure Protection**: IP68 Submersible up to 30m depth
- **Quality System**: ISO 9001:2015

## Applications
Coal washeries at Coal India, mine dewatering in Odisha, slag sumps at JSW Steel.
