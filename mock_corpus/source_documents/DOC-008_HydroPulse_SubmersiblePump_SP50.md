# Technical Specification: HydroPulse SP50 Slurry Submersible Pump
**Manufacturer**: HydroPulse Heavy Machinery
**Series**: SP50 Mining & Slurry
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40151513 (Submersible pumps)

## Technical Specifications
- **Discharge Diameter**: 4 inch (DN100) Flanged
- **Max Flow Capacity**: 1200 GPM (272 m³/h)
- **Max Head**: 140 ft (42.6 meters)
- **Max Solids Passage**: 1.5 inch (38 mm) spherical solids
- **Motor Rating**: 30 kW (40 HP) 460V 3-Phase 60Hz
- **Operating Temperature**: 0°C to 50°C (32°F to 122°F continuous)
- **Impeller Material**: High-Chrome White Iron (27% Cr, Hardness 650 HB)
- **Volute Casing**: High-Chrome Iron with Abrasion Resistant Rubber Liner
- **Mechanical Seal**: Double Mechanical Seal in Oil Chamber (SiC/SiC)

## Compliance & Standards
- **Explosion Safety**: MSHA Approved / ATEX Ex d IIB T4 Mb (Mining)
- **Enclosure Protection**: IP68 Submersible up to 30m depth
- **Quality System**: ISO 9001:2015

## Applications
Mine dewatering, sand & gravel extraction, industrial lagoon dredging, mill scale sumps.
