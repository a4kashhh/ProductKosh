# Product Spec: Instrumentation Ltd Forged Steel Gate Valve
**Manufacturer**: Instrumentation Limited Palakkad (ILP India)
**Series**: GV900 Forged Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141603 (Gate valves)

## Technical Specifications
- **Valve Size**: 15 mm to 50 mm (1/2 inch to 2 inch)
- **Pressure Rating**: Class 800, Class 1500 / PN 100 to PN 250
- **Max Operating Pressure**: 255 Bar (3705 PSI)
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -29°C to 450°C
- **Body & Bonnet Material**: ASTM A105 Forged Carbon Steel / A182 F316 SS
- **Trim Material**: 13% Cr Stainless Steel with Stellite 6 Facing
- **End Connections**: Threaded NPT / Socket Weld (SW)

## Compliance & Standards
- **Indian Boiler Regulations**: IBR Form III-C Certified Gate Valve
- **Design Standard**: API 602 / BS 5352
- **Testing Standard**: API 598 Inspection

## Applications
High-pressure drain and vent lines in Indian thermal power plants, refinery hydrocarbon sampling lines.
