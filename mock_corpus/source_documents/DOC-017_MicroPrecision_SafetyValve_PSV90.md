# Technical Manual: Micro Precision Pressure Safety Relief Valve
**Manufacturer**: Micro Precision Calibration India
**Series**: PSV90 Safety Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141604 (Safety relief valves)

## Technical Specifications
- **Inlet Size**: 25 mm to 200 mm (1 inch to 8 inch) Flanged ANSI 150# to 2500#
- **Set Pressure Range**: 1.5 Bar to 250 Bar (21.7 PSI to 3625 PSI)
- **Orifice Letter Designation**: Orifice D through Orifice T per API 526
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -45°C to 538°C
- **Overpressure**: 10% for Steam and Gas
- **Body Material**: Cast Carbon Steel WCB / Chrome-Moly WC6 / CF8M SS

## Compliance & Standards
- **Indian Boiler Regulations**: IBR Form III-C Approved Pressure Relief Valve
- **ASME Code**: ASME BPV Code Section VIII Division 1 (UV Stamp)
- **Design Standard**: API 526 / API 520

## Applications
Overpressure protection on steam boilers at BHEL, gas oil separators at ONGC, chemical pressure vessels.
