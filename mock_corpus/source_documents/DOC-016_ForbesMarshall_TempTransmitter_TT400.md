# Product Specification: Forbes Marshall Smart Temperature Transmitter
**Manufacturer**: Forbes Marshall India
**Product Series**: TT400 Universal Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 41112200 (Temperature measuring instruments)

## Technical Specifications
- **Input Types**: Universal RTD (Pt100, Pt1000) & Thermocouple (Types B, E, J, K, N, R, S, T)
- **Accuracy**: ±0.1°C for Pt100 RTD
- **Output Signal**: 4-20 mA Loop-Powered with HART 7 Protocol
- **Supply Voltage**: 8.0 to 35 VDC
- **Galvanic Isolation**: 1500 VAC Input/Output Isolation
- **Ambient Operating Temp**: -40°C to 85°C

## Compliance & Standards
- **PESO Approval**: PESO Approved Intrinsically Safe Ex ia IIC T4/T6 Ga
- **EMC Compliance**: EN 61326-1 / NAMUR NE21
- **Functional Safety**: SIL 2 / SIL 3 Certified per IEC 61508

## Applications
Thermowell assembly integration in Indian refineries, pharmaceutical reactor vessel control.
