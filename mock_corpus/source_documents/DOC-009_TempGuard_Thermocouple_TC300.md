# Technical Datasheet: TempGuard Industrial Duplex Thermocouple
**Manufacturer**: TempGuard Sensors India Pvt Ltd
**Product Series**: TC300 High-Temp Industrial
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 41112213 (Thermocouples)

## Technical Specifications
- **Thermocouple Calibration**: Type K (Chromel/Alumel) / Type N
- **Measurement Temperature Range**: -200°C to 1260°C
- **Accuracy Class**: Special Tolerances Class 1 per IS 2053 (±1.1°C)
- **Sheath Diameter**: 6.35 mm (1/4") / 9.53 mm (3/8")
- **Sheath Material**: Inconel 600 / 310 SS
- **Immersion Length**: 300 mm to 1000 mm
- **Connection Head**: Die-Cast Aluminum Explosion Proof Head (Form B)

## Compliance & Standards
- **Calibration Standard**: BIS IS 2053 / IEC 60584-1
- **Traceability**: NABL Accredited Calibration Certificate Included
- **Hazardous Locations**: PESO / CCOE Approved Ex d IIC T6
- **Environmental**: RoHS Compliant

## Applications
Steel reheating furnace temperature monitoring at SAIL, heat treatment autoclaves.
