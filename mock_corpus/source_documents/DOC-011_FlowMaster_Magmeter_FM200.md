# Technical Specification: FlowMaster FM200 Electromagnetic Flowmeter
**Manufacturer**: FlowMaster Controls India
**Product Series**: FM200 Industrial Magmeter
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 41112501 (Flow meters)

## Technical Specifications
- **Pipe Size Range**: DN25 to DN300 (1 inch to 12 inch)
- **Flow Velocity Range**: 0.1 m/s to 10 m/s
- **Volumetric Flow Accuracy**: ±0.2% of Measured Value
- **Fluid Conductivity Limit**: > 5 μS/cm
- **Max Pressure Rating**: 40 Bar (PN 40 / ANSI 150#)
- **Fluid Temperature Range**: -20°C to 150°C
- **Lining Material**: PTFE / PFA / Hard Rubber
- **Electrode Material**: 316L SS / Hastelloy C-276
- **Protection Class**: Sensor IP68 Submersible (10m continuous)

## Compliance & Standards
- **Water Approvals**: BIS IS 779 / Central Ground Water Authority (CGWA) Compliant
- **Hazardous Area**: PESO Approved Ex eb mb IIC T4

## Applications
Smart Cities drinking water distribution, industrial effluent monitoring for CPCB compliance.
