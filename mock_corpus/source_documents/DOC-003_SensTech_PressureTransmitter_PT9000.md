# Engineering Specification: SensTech PT9000 Smart Pressure Transmitter
**Manufacturer**: SensTech Measurement Solutions
**Product Series**: PT9000 Precision Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 41112404 (Pressure transmitters)

## Technical Specifications
- **Pressure Range**: 0.1 Bar to 400 Bar (1.45 PSI to 5800 PSI) Gauge & Absolute
- **Calibrated Span Accuracy**: ±0.04% of URL (Upper Range Limit)
- **Long-term Stability**: ±0.05% URL per 10 years
- **Output Signal**: 4-20 mA DC with HART Protocol 7.0 / Foundation Fieldbus
- **Power Supply**: 10.5 to 45 VDC
- **Operating Ambient Temperature**: -40°C to 85°C (-40°F to 185°F)
- **Process Wetted Diaphragm**: Hastelloy C-276 / Tantalum / 316L SS
- **Fill Fluid**: Silicone Oil / Fluorolube (Oxygen Service)
- **Enclosure Rating**: NEMA 4X / IP66 & IP67 Die-Cast Aluminum

## Compliance & Standards
- **Intrinsic Safety**: FM Approved Class I Div 1, ATEX II 1G Ex ia IIC T4/T6 Ga
- **Explosion Proof**: IECEx Ex db IIC T6 Gb
- **Functional Safety**: SIL 2 / SIL 3 Certified per IEC 61508
- **Environmental**: RoHS 3 (EU 2015/863) Compliant, REACH Compliant

## Applications
Oil & gas pipeline monitoring, chemical reactor pressure control, boiler steam pressure measurement.
