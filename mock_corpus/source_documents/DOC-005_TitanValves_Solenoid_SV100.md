# Product Datasheet: Titan SV100 Explosion-Proof Solenoid Valve
**Manufacturer**: Titan Valves & Controls
**Product Series**: SV100 Direct-Acting Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141605 (Solenoid valves)

## Technical Specifications
- **Port Size**: 1/2 inch NPT Female (DN15)
- **Valve Function**: 2/2-Way Normally Closed (NC) / Normally Open (NO)
- **Operating Pressure Differential**: 0 to 150 PSI (0 to 10.3 Bar)
- **Flow Coefficient (Cv)**: 4.5
- **Operating Voltage Options**: 24 VDC (8.5 Watt) / 120 VAC 60Hz (11 Watt)
- **Response Time**: < 20 ms Opening, < 30 ms Closing
- **Body Material**: 316L Stainless Steel Barstock
- **Seal Material**: FKM (Viton) / NBR / PTFE High Temperature
- **Operating Temperature**: -10°C to 120°C (14°F to 248°F)

## Compliance & Standards
- **Electrical Enclosure**: NEMA 4, 4X, 7, 9 Explosion Proof
- **Hazardous Approvals**: UL Listed Class I Div 1 Groups B, C, D; ATEX II 2G Ex db IIC T5 Gb
- **Ingress Protection**: IP67
- **Safety Rating**: SIL 3 Capable

## Applications
Burner fuel gas shutoff, automated actuator pilot controls, offshore skid emergency shutdown.
