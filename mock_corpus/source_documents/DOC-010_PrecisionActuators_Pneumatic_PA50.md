# Product Manual: Precision Actuators Pneumatic Valve Actuator
**Manufacturer**: Precision Actuators India Pvt Ltd
**Series**: PA50 Double Acting / Spring Return
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141901 (Pneumatic actuators)

## Technical Specifications
- **Torque Output**: 520 Nm at 6 Bar air supply pressure
- **Air Supply Pressure Range**: 2 Bar to 8 Bar (29 PSI to 116 PSI)
- **Rotation Angle**: 90° ± 5° Stroke Adjustment
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -40°C to 80°C
- **Body Material**: Extruded Aluminum Alloy Hard Anodized (40 microns)
- **Mounting Interface**: ISO 5211 F07/F10 & NAMUR VDI/VDE 3845

## Compliance & Standards
- **Valve Interface**: ISO 5211 Standard
- **Explosion Safety**: PESO / CCOE Approved (Ex II 2GD c T6)
- **Safety Integrity**: SIL 3 Capable per IEC 61508

## Applications
Quarter-turn valve automation (ball valves, butterfly valves, plug valves) across Indian chemical plants.
