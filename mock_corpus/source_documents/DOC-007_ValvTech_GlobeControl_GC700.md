# Product Datasheet: ValvTech GC700 Heavy-Duty Control Globe Valve
**Manufacturer**: ValvTech Engineering
**Product Series**: GC700 Control Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141602 (Control valves)

## Technical Specifications
- **Valve Size**: 1 inch to 8 inch (DN25 - DN200)
- **Pressure Class**: ANSI Class 150, 300, 600
- **Flow Characteristic**: Equal Percentage, Linear, Quick Opening
- **Trim Cv Range**: 0.5 to 450 Cv
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -40°C to 425°C (-40°F to 797°F)
- **Max Pressure Drop**: 720 PSI (49.6 Bar)
- **Body Material**: Cast Steel A216 WCB / Stainless Steel A351 CF8M
- **Trim Material**: Hardened 17-4PH Stainless Steel with Cobalt-Base Alloy
- **Actuator**: Pneumatic Diaphragm with Smart HART Digital Positioner

## Compliance & Standards
- **Design Standard**: ASME B16.34 / ISA 75.01.01
- **Seat Leakage**: ANSI/FCI 70-2 Class IV (Standard), Class V (Optional)
- **Fugitive Emission**: EPA Method 21 / ISO 15848-1
- **Safety Rating**: SIL 2 Certified per IEC 61508

## Applications
Feedwater control, boiler blowdown, pressure regulation stations in chemical plants.
