# Product Specification: Apex MP120 High-Pressure Multistage Centrifugal Pump
**Manufacturer**: Apex Fluid Systems
**Product Line**: MP120 Multistage Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40151503 (Centrifugal pumps)

## Technical Specifications
- **Max Flow Rate**: 350 GPM (79.5 m³/h)
- **Max Discharge Pressure**: 580 PSI (40 Bar / 400m Head)
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -15°C to 120°C (5°F to 248°F)
- **Motor Power Rating**: 25 HP to 150 HP (18.5 kW to 110 kW)
- **Number of Stages**: 4 to 16 Stages
- **Pump Shaft & Impellers**: AISI 316 Stainless Steel
- **Intermediate Chamber Material**: Cast Stainless Steel CF8M
- **Shaft Seal**: Cartridge Mechanical Seal Silicon Carbide vs Silicon Carbide (SiC/SiC)

## Compliance & Standards
- **Design Standard**: ISO 9905 / DIN 24255
- **Boiler Feed Standard**: ASME Section I / Section VIII compatible
- **Efficiency**: IE3 / IE4 Premium Efficiency Motors
- **Quality Certification**: ISO 9001:2015

## Applications
Boiler feedwater supply, reverse osmosis desalination high pressure feed, high-pressure washing.
