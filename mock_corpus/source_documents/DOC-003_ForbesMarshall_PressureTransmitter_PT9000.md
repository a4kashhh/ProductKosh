# Engineering Specification: Forbes Marshall PT9000 Smart Pressure Transmitter
**Manufacturer**: Forbes Marshall India
**Product Series**: PT9000 Precision Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 41112404 (Pressure transmitters)

## Technical Specifications
- **Pressure Range**: 0.1 Bar to 400 Bar (1.0 kg/cm² to 408 kg/cm²) Gauge & Absolute
- **Calibrated Span Accuracy**: ±0.04% of URL
- **Output Signal**: 4-20 mA DC with HART Protocol 7.0 / RS485 Modbus
- **Power Supply**: 10.5 to 45 VDC
- **Operating Ambient Temperature**: -40°C to 85°C
- **Process Wetted Diaphragm**: Hastelloy C-276 / Tantalum / 316L SS
- **Enclosure Rating**: IP67 / NEMA 4X Die-Cast Aluminum Housing

## Compliance & Standards
- **Indian Safety Approvals**: PESO / CCOE Approved Explosive Atmosphere (Ex d IIC T6)
- **Mine Safety**: DGMS (Directorate General of Mines Safety) Certified
- **Functional Safety**: SIL 2 / SIL 3 Certified per IEC 61508
- **Environmental**: RoHS Compliant, BIS Registered

## Applications
GAIL gas pipeline monitoring, RCF chemical reactor pressure control, Tata Steel boiler steam pressure.
