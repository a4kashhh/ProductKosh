# Technical Spec: OmniFlow Clamp-On Ultrasonic Flowmeter
**Manufacturer**: OmniFlow India Process Technologies
**Series**: US300 Non-Invasive Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 41112501 (Flow meters)

## Technical Specifications
- **Pipe Diameter Range**: 15 mm to 5000 mm (1/2 inch to 200 inch)
- **Flow Velocity Range**: ±0.01 m/s to ±12 m/s
- **Volumetric Accuracy**: ±0.5% of reading
- **Fluid Temperature Range**: -40°C to 160°C
- **Transducer Mounting**: Clamp-On Non-Invasive
- **Signal Output**: 4-20mA, RS485 Modbus RTU

## Compliance & Standards
- **Enclosure Protection**: IP65 Transmitter, IP68 Transducers (Submersible 100m)
- **Hazardous Approvals**: PESO Approved Zone 2 Ex ec IIC T4 Gc

## Applications
District heating water metering, pipe flow verification without shutdown in Indian municipal water boards.
