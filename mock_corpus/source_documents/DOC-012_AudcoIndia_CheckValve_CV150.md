# Engineering Datasheet: Audco Dual-Plate Wafer Check Valve
**Manufacturer**: Audco India Limited
**Series**: CV150 Dual Plate
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141601 (Check valves)

## Technical Specifications
- **Size Range**: 50 mm to 400 mm (2 inch to 16 inch)
- **Pressure Rating**: PN 16, PN 25, PN 40 / ANSI Class 150, 300
- **Max Operating Pressure**: 51 Bar (740 PSI)
- **Temperature Limits**: -29°C to 230°C
- **Cracking Pressure**: 0.01 Bar (0.15 PSI)
- **Body Material**: Ductile Iron IS 1865 / Cast Carbon Steel IS 210 / SS316
- **Plate / Disc Material**: ASTM A351 CF8M Stainless Steel
- **Spring Material**: Inconel X-750 High Strength Alloy

## Compliance & Standards
- **Indian Standard**: BIS IS 5312 Swing Check Valves / IBR Approved
- **Testing Standard**: API 598 Inspection & Testing
- **Quality Assurance**: ISO 9001:2015

## Applications
Preventing backflow in pump discharge lines, HVAC chilled water, seawater cooling lines at Adani Mundra.
