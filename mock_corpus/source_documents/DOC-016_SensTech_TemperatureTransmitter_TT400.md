# Product Specification: SensTech TT400 Head-Mount Smart Temperature Transmitter
**Manufacturer**: SensTech Measurement Solutions
**Product Series**: TT400 Universal Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 41112200 (Temperature measuring instruments)

## Technical Specifications
- **Input Types**: Universal RTD (Pt100, Pt1000, Ni100) & Thermocouple (Types B, E, J, K, N, R, S, T)
- **Accuracy**: ±0.1°C (±0.18°F) for Pt100 RTD
- **Output Signal**: 4-20 mA Loop-Powered with HART 7 Protocol
- **Supply Voltage**: 8.0 to 35 VDC
- **Galvanic Isolation**: 1500 VAC Input/Output Isolation
- **Ambient Operating Temp**: -40°C to 85°C (-40°F to 185°F)
- **Housing Form Factor**: DIN Form B Head Mounting / Rail Mount

## Compliance & Standards
- **Intrinsic Safety**: ATEX II 1G Ex ia IIC T4/T6 Ga / CSA Class I Div 1
- **EMC Compliance**: EN 61326-1 / NAMUR NE21
- **Functional Safety**: SIL 2 / SIL 3 Certified per IEC 61508
- **Environmental**: RoHS 3, REACH

## Applications
Thermowell assembly integration, process vessel temperature monitoring, HVAC plant controls.
