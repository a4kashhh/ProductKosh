# Technical Specification: OmniFlow CM50 Industrial Coriolis Mass Flow Meter
**Manufacturer**: OmniFlow India Process Technologies
**Product Line**: CM50 High Accuracy Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 41112501 (Flow meters)

## Technical Specifications
- **Nominal Line Size**: DN25 to DN80 (1 inch to 3 inch)
- **Mass Flow Measurement Range**: 50 kg/h to 50,000 kg/h
- **Mass Flow Accuracy**: ±0.1% of rate (Liquids), ±0.5% of rate (Gases)
- **Density Measurement Range**: 0.001 to 3.0 g/cm³
- **Max Operating Pressure**: 100 Bar (102 kg/cm²)
- **Process Temperature Range**: -50°C to 200°C
- **Sensor Wetted Material**: Titanium Grade 2 / 316L SS
- **Transmitter Enclosure**: Dual Compartment Aluminum IP67

## Compliance & Standards
- **Legal Metrology**: Legal Metrology India / W&M Approved for Custody Transfer
- **Hazardous Locations**: PESO Approved Ex db ia IIC T6 Gb
- **Sanitary Standard**: EHEDG & 3-A Sanitary Certified
- **Quality**: ISO 9001:2015

Milk & dairy flow batching at Amul Anand, chemical dosing at Atul Chemicals, biofuel transfer.
