# Product Specification: KSB MP120 High-Pressure Multistage Centrifugal Pump
**Manufacturer**: KSB Pumps Limited India
**Product Line**: MP120 Multistage Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40151503 (Centrifugal pumps)

## Technical Specifications
- **Max Flow Rate**: 79.5 m³/h (350 GPM)
- **Max Discharge Pressure**: 40 Bar (580 PSI / 400m Head)
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -15°C to 120°C
- **Motor Power Rating**: 18.5 kW to 110 kW (25 HP to 150 HP)
- **Number of Stages**: 4 to 16 Stages
- **Pump Shaft & Impellers**: AISI 316 Stainless Steel
- **Shaft Seal**: Cartridge Mechanical Seal SiC/SiC

## Compliance & Standards
- **Boiler Compliance**: IBR (Indian Boiler Regulations) 1950 Boiler Feed Approved
- **Design Standard**: ISO 9905 / DIN 24255
- **Motor Efficiency**: IE3 Premium Efficiency Motors

## Applications
Boiler feedwater supply at thermal power stations, reverse osmosis desalination, high-pressure washing.
