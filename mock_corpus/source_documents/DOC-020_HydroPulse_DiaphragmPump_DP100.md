# Technical Datasheet: HydroPulse DP100 Air-Operated Double Diaphragm (AODD) Pump
**Manufacturer**: HydroPulse Heavy Machinery
**Series**: DP100 Metallic AODD Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40151504 (Positive displacement pumps)

## Technical Specifications
- **Fluid Port Connection**: 2 inch NPT / BSP Female (DN50)
- **Max Flow Capacity**: 150 GPM (568 L/min)
- **Max Air Supply Pressure**: 125 PSI (8.6 Bar)
- **Max Displacement per Stroke**: 0.46 Gallons (1.7 Liters)
- **Max Suspended Solids**: 0.25 inch (6.4 mm)
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -12°C to 107°C (10°F to 225°F)
- **Fluid Section Material**: 316 Stainless Steel / Aluminum / Cast Iron
- **Diaphragm & Ball Material**: PTFE (Teflon) with Santoprene Backer

## Compliance & Standards
- **Explosion Safety**: ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU (Group II 2GD c TX)
- **Sanitary Compliance**: FDA Compliant Materials (21 CFR 177)
- **Quality System**: ISO 9001:2015

## Applications
Chemical transfer, paint and ink circulation, drum emptying, solvent recovery.
