# Technical Manual: UltraLevel 80GHz Non-Contact Radar Level Transmitter
**Manufacturer**: UltraLevel Sensors India
**Product Series**: RL95 80GHz Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 41111900 (Level measuring instruments)

## Technical Specifications
- **Measuring Range**: 0.5 meter to 120 meters
- **Radar Frequency**: 80 GHz FMCW (77-81 GHz)
- **Measurement Accuracy**: ±1.0 mm
- **Beam Angle**: 3° Narrow Beam
- **Process Pressure Range**: -1 Bar to 160 Bar
- **Process Temperature Range**: -196°C to 450°C
- **Antenna Material**: PEEK / PTFE Lens / 316L SS Flange

## Compliance & Standards
- **Explosion Safety**: PESO Approved Ex ia IIC T6 Ga
- **Safety Rating**: SIL 2 / SIL 3 Redundant per IEC 61508
- **Enclosure Protection**: IP68 Stainless Steel Housing

## Applications
Tall grain silo monitoring at FCI (Food Corporation of India), chemical storage tanks at IPCL.
