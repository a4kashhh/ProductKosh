# Technical Manual: Audco India Triple-Offset Butterfly Valve
**Manufacturer**: Audco India Limited
**Product Series**: BF400 High Performance
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141615 (Butterfly valves)

## Technical Specifications
- **Valve Size Range**: 80 mm to 600 mm (3 inch to 24 inch)
- **Pressure Rating**: PN 16, PN 25, PN 40, PN 100 / ANSI Class 150, 300, 600
- **Max Operating Pressure**: 102 Bar (104 kg/cm²)
- **Temperature Rating**: -196°C Cryogenic to +450°C High Temp
- **Body & Disc Material**: ASTM A351 CF8M Stainless Steel / IS 210 Gr FG 260
- **Shaft Material**: 17-4PH Stainless Steel
- **Seat Ring**: Laminated SS316 with Graphite / Stellite 6 Solid Seat
- **Tightness Class**: ISO 5208 Rate A Zero Leakage Bi-Directional

## Compliance & Standards
- **Indian Standard**: BIS IS 13095 Butterfly Valves for General Purposes / IBR Approved
- **Fire Safe Standard**: API 607 6th Edition / ISO 10497
- **Fugitive Emissions**: ISO 15848-1 Class BH
- **PESO Approval**: PESO Certified for Hazardous Hydrocarbon Service

## Applications
Kochi LNG terminal, HPCL refinery high-temp exhaust gas isolation, Adani power cooling loops.
