# Product Datasheet: Instrumentation Ltd Heavy-Duty Control Globe Valve
**Manufacturer**: Instrumentation Limited Palakkad (ILP India)
**Product Series**: GC700 Control Series
**UNSPSC Taxonomy**: 40141602 (Control valves)

## Technical Specifications
- **Valve Size**: 25 mm to 200 mm (1 inch to 8 inch)
- **Pressure Rating**: ANSI Class 150, 300, 600 / PN 16 to PN 100
- **Flow Characteristic**: Equal Percentage, Linear
- **Trim Cv Range**: 0.5 to 450 Cv
- **Operating Temperature Range**: -40°C to 425°C
- **Max Pressure Drop**: 49.6 Bar (720 PSI)
- **Body Material**: Cast Carbon Steel WCB / Stainless Steel CF8M
- **Trim Material**: Hardened 17-4PH SS with Stellite 6 Facing
- **Actuator**: Pneumatic Diaphragm with Smart HART Digital Positioner

## Compliance & Standards
- **Indian Boiler Regulations**: IBR Form III-C Certified
- **Design Standard**: ASME B16.34 / IS 10182
- **Seat Leakage**: ANSI/FCI 70-2 Class IV / Class V
- **Safety Rating**: SIL 2 Certified per IEC 61508

## Applications
Feedwater control at BHEL thermal plants, boiler blowdown, pressure regulation in fertilizer units.
